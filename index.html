Fuel prices are climbing. Your route doesn't have to stop.

ElectroCocha helps transport workers and drivers in Cochabamba understand what switching to electric could cost, how it could be financed, and which incentives may help.

Get in touch

The problem

Bolivia is facing severe diesel and petrol shortages. After fuel subsidies were removed, diesel prices rose by more than 160%. For a bus or taxi operator, fuel is a major daily cost, so every price jump lands on the people who keep the city moving.

Long queues, blockades and an unreliable supply make it worse. A vehicle that cannot refuel cannot earn.

Transport workers in Cochabamba carry this pressure, and many do not have clear, simple information about the alternatives.

The solution

ElectroCocha explains the switch to electric in plain language, so people can decide for themselves.

  • Costs: compare the running costs of diesel and electric vehicles with our calculator.
  • Financing: understand the financing schemes proposed for transport operators.
  • Incentives: learn about the tax incentives proposed for electric vehicles.

The city has proposed electric public transport as a choice for transport workers, not a requirement. ElectroCocha exists to make that choice an informed one.

Who it is for

  • Bus and taxi operators: weighing the cost of switching.
  • Private drivers: looking for a way around fuel shortages.
  • Commuters: who rely on affordable, dependable transport.
  • Local government: planning the move to electric transport.
