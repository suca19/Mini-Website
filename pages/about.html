About us

Keeping Cochabamba moving, with less fuel worry

We help transport workers and drivers understand what switching to electric really means for them, in plain language.

Try the cost calculator

Our mission

Our mission is to give the people who keep Cochabamba moving clear, honest information about electric vehicles. We explain costs, financing and incentives in plain language, so that bus and taxi operators, drivers and commuters can make their own informed choice.

We do not tell anyone to switch. We help people work out whether switching makes sense for them.

How it works

  1. Enter your figures in the cost calculator.
  2. Compare diesel and electric running costs.
  3. Get in touch with your questions about financing and incentives.

Who is behind ElectroCocha

ElectroCocha is a student prototype, designed and built as a college web development project for a client in Cochabamba. It is not a company, and it does not sell vehicles, loans or charging.

The project is built in short sprints. At the end of each one, the client reviews the site and gives feedback, so it can keep up as fuel policy and incentives change.

Partners

Partners are not confirmed yet. In the future we would like to work with transport unions, local government, electricity providers and vehicle sellers, so the information on this site can be checked and kept up to date.
