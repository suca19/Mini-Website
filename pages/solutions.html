Your options for going electric

Electric vehicle options

Different jobs need different vehicles. These are the main options.

  • Electric buses and minibuses

    For micro and trufi operators who drive set routes every day.

    • Fixed routes make charging easy to plan
    • Usually charged overnight at a depot
    • Highest price, but the biggest fuel savings

  • Electric cars and taxis

    For taxi drivers and private drivers.

    • Can often charge from a home socket
    • Quiet and simple to drive
    • Check the range against your longest day

  • Electric motorbikes

    For deliveries and short trips around the city.

    • Lowest purchase price
    • Some have batteries you can take out and charge indoors
    • Best for short distances

Possible benefits

  • Lower running costs

    Use the calculator to compare with your own figures.

  • Less dependence on fuel

    Electric vehicles do not need diesel or petrol, so fuel queues do not stop them.

  • Cleaner, quieter streets

    No exhaust fumes from the vehicle, and much less engine noise.

  • Financing and incentives

    Financing schemes and tax incentives have been proposed for transport operators.

Financing and incentives

The biggest barrier to switching is the price of the vehicle. Two kinds of help have been proposed for transport operators:

  • Financing schemes: loans designed for transport operators, so the cost of a vehicle can be spread over time.
  • Tax incentives: lower taxes on electric vehicles, to bring their price closer to diesel ones.

These are proposals and can change. Before you sign anything, ask:

  1. What is the total cost, including interest, over the whole loan?
  2. Could the monthly fuel saving from the calculator cover the monthly payment?
  3. What does the warranty cover, especially the battery?
  4. Where will I charge, and how long will it take?

Not sure where to start? Send us your question and choose "Financing options" or "Incentives".

Where can I charge?

Most drivers charge overnight at home or at a depot. For longer days, public charging points help.
